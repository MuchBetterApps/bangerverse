# Waitlist · your spot

A giant position number and a concrete way to move up by referring friends.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="Waitlist · your spot preview">

- **Its job:** Turn one waitlist signup into several.
- **Send it:** Right after joining a waitlist, then when the position changes.
- **Why it works:** A concrete position plus a concrete way to move up. Specific numbers make referral asks work.
- **Measure:** Referrals per signup
- **Keep:** the number; the referral link
- **Avoid:** vague 'we'll be in touch'

**Subject lines to test**

- You're #{{position}}. Want to skip ahead?
- Your spot on the list
- 3 friends and you're in next week

Slots: `preheader`, `position`, `summary`, `referral_offer`, `referral_detail`, `referral_link`, `cta_label`, `cta_url`
