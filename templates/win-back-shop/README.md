# We miss you · shop

A warm note, a thank-you offer, and new pieces since their last order.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="We miss you · shop preview">

- **Its job:** Get a lapsed customer to order again.
- **Send it:** 90–120 days after the last order.
- **Why it works:** Novelty (what's new since they last came) plus a thank-you, not a desperate discount.
- **Measure:** Repeat orders within 14 days
- **Keep:** new items; a modest offer
- **Avoid:** 'we miss you' guilt; deep discounts

**Subject lines to test**

- It's been a while, {{first_name|there}}
- New since your last order
- A thank-you for coming back
