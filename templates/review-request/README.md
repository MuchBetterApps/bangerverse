# Rate us in one tap

Stars in the email: happy customers go to a public review, unhappy ones to you.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="Rate us in one tap preview">

- **Its job:** Collect public reviews, and catch unhappy customers privately first.
- **Send it:** 7 days after delivery or a completed service.
- **Why it works:** Stars as links: 4–5 route to your public review page, 1–3 to a private feedback form so you can fix it.
- **Measure:** Public reviews per 100 orders
- **Keep:** stars as one-click links; short copy
- **Avoid:** asking before they've used it

**Subject lines to test**

- How was {{thing_reviewed}}?
- Two seconds: rate your order
- {{first_name|there}}, how did we do?

Slots: `preheader`, `product_image`, `thing_reviewed`, `order_note`, `answer_url_1`, `answer_url_2`, `answer_url_3`, `answer_url_4`, `answer_url_5`, `followup`
