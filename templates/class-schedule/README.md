# This week's schedule

A timetable for studios, gyms and workshops, with spots left and one-click booking.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="This week's schedule preview">

- **Its job:** Fill classes for the week.
- **Send it:** Weekly, on Sunday or Monday.
- **Why it works:** An at-a-glance timetable with real 'spots left' counts makes booking a two-second decision.
- **Measure:** Bookings from the email
- **Keep:** times; spots left
- **Avoid:** more than eight rows

**Subject lines to test**

- This week at {{company_name}}
- Two classes almost full
- Your week, planned
