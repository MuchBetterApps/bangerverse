# Invitation

A personal invitation that leads with the person who sent it.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="Invitation preview">

- **Its job:** Get the invitee to accept.
- **Send it:** When a member invites someone.
- **Why it works:** Leads with a face and a name the person knows; their note (when given) outweighs any product pitch.
- **Measure:** Invite acceptance within 72h
- **Keep:** inviter name; their note
- **Avoid:** feature lists

**Subject lines to test**

- {{inviter_name}} invited you to {{workspace_name}}
- Join {{inviter_name}} on {{company_name}}
- {{inviter_name}} wants you in {{workspace_name}}

Slots: `preheader`, `inviter_initials`, `workspace_initials`, `inviter_name`, `workspace_name`, `invite_note`, `cta_label`, `cta_url`
