# Monthly report

Their own numbers with change on last month, a small chart, and one highlight.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="Monthly report preview">

- **Its job:** Remind people of the value they already got.
- **Send it:** First weekday of each month.
- **Why it works:** Personal numbers with a trend give credit to the person; a highlight makes it a story, not a spreadsheet.
- **Measure:** Sessions within 3 days of send
- **Keep:** their numbers; one highlight
- **Avoid:** more than four stats

**Subject lines to test**

- Your {{period}}: {{headline_stat}}
- {{first_name|there}}, your month in numbers
- Up 38% on last month
