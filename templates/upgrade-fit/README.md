# You've outgrown your plan

Usage evidence first, then the two plans side by side.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="You've outgrown your plan preview">

- **Its job:** Upgrade heavy users on the lower plan.
- **Send it:** When usage crosses ~80% of the plan for 2 periods.
- **Why it works:** Evidence first ('you did X'), then a fair comparison. People upgrade when it's their own conclusion.
- **Measure:** Upgrades within 14 days
- **Keep:** their usage number; side-by-side
- **Avoid:** threatening limits

**Subject lines to test**

- You sent {{usage_value}} this month
- You've outgrown Starter
- Room to grow: Pro
