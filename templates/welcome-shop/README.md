# Welcome · shop

A welcome gift code, the story in one line, and bestsellers with photos.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="Welcome · shop preview">

- **Its job:** Turn a new subscriber into a first order.
- **Send it:** Immediately after joining the list.
- **Why it works:** The promised welcome offer up front, then social proof by showing bestsellers ('what people love').
- **Measure:** First-order rate within 14 days
- **Keep:** the code; the expiry; bestseller photos
- **Avoid:** more than four products; a second discount

**Subject lines to test**

- Welcome to {{company_name}}: 10% off your first order
- Your welcome gift is inside
- Here's what everyone buys first
