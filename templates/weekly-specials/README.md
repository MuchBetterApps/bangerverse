# This week's specials

A menu card for restaurants, cafés and bakeries: dishes, prices, and the hours.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="This week's specials preview">

- **Its job:** Fill tables (or the counter) this week.
- **Send it:** Weekly, early in the week.
- **Why it works:** A menu feels like an invitation, not an ad; limited-time specials give a reason to come this week.
- **Measure:** Bookings and visits
- **Keep:** dishes with prices; hours
- **Avoid:** long stories

**Subject lines to test**

- This week: {{special_1_name}} and more
- Three specials until Sunday
- What's cooking this week
