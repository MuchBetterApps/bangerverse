# Stuck? Can I help

A behavior-triggered personal offer of 15 minutes, with no feature list.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="Stuck? Can I help preview">

- **Its job:** Rescue signups who stalled.
- **Send it:** 3 quiet days after signup without the key action.
- **Why it works:** Human help at the moment of friction converts better than another tips email.
- **Measure:** Replies and booked calls
- **Keep:** personal tone; calendar link as a text link
- **Avoid:** buttons; images; feature lists

**Subject lines to test**

- stuck on something?
- can I help, {{first_name|there}}?
- 15 minutes?

Slots: `preheader`, `message`, `offer`, `calendar_url`, `calendar_label`, `alternative`, `sender_initials`, `sender_name`, `sender_role`
