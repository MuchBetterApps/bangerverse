# Impact report

What supporters made possible: big numbers, one story, and a thank-you.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="Impact report preview">

- **Its job:** Show supporters their impact so they give again.
- **Send it:** Quarterly or yearly.
- **Why it works:** Credit the reader ('you made this'), make impact concrete, then invite the next step softly.
- **Measure:** Repeat donations and replies
- **Keep:** numbers credited to supporters; one story
- **Avoid:** an ask in the first half

**Subject lines to test**

- What you made possible in {{period}}
- 1,000 trees, thanks to you
- Your {{period}} impact report
