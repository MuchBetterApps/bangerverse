# We saved your spot

Opens on the last thing they worked on, then what's new, with one click back into it.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="We saved your spot preview">

- **Its job:** Bring a lapsed user back to their own work.
- **Send it:** After 30–60 days of inactivity.
- **Why it works:** Their own unfinished work (Zeigarnik effect) is a stronger pull than any feature news.
- **Measure:** Return visits within 7 days
- **Keep:** their last item; one-click return
- **Avoid:** guilt

**Subject lines to test**

- {{last_item}} is where you left it
- We saved your spot, {{first_name|there}}
- A lot changed since you left

Slots: `preheader`, `last_item`, `last_item_state`, `cta_label`, `cta_url`, `change_1_title`, `change_1_body`, `change_2_title`, `change_2_body`, `change_3_title`, `change_3_body`
