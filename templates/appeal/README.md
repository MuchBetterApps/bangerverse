# Fundraising appeal

Progress toward a goal, what each amount does, and a story of one person helped.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="Fundraising appeal preview">

- **Its job:** Raise donations for a specific goal.
- **Send it:** Campaign launch, midpoint, final 48 hours.
- **Why it works:** Goal gradient (visible progress pulls people to help finish) plus concrete impact per amount.
- **Measure:** Donations and average gift
- **Keep:** the progress bar; impact per amount; one real story
- **Avoid:** guilt; vague asks

**Subject lines to test**

- We're {{percent}}% of the way there
- $25 plants a tree
- 48 hours left to reach our goal

Slots: `preheader`, `hero_image`, `hero_alt`, `campaign_name`, `percent`, `raised`, `goal`, `story`, `impact_1`, `amount_1`, `impact_2`, `amount_2`, `impact_3`, `amount_3`, `cta_label`, `cta_url`, `tax_note`
