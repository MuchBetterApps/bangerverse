# Course launch

What they'll learn module by module, the price, a student quote, and enrollment dates.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="Course launch preview">

- **Its job:** Sell seats in a course or cohort.
- **Send it:** Open cart, mid-point, and last day.
- **Why it works:** Concrete outcomes per module, social proof, and a real close date. The curriculum sells the course.
- **Measure:** Enrollments
- **Keep:** modules; price; close date; a real testimonial
- **Avoid:** income claims

**Subject lines to test**

- Doors are open: {{course_name}}
- Six weeks to {{outcome}}
- Enrollment closes Friday
