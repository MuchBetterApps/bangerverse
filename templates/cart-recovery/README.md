# Left in your cart

The product with its photo, three reassurances, and no discount on the first touch.

<img src="preview.webp" width="420" alt="Left in your cart preview">

- **Its job:** Recover abandoned checkouts.
- **Send it:** 1 hour after abandonment; a second touch at 24h may add an offer.
- **Why it works:** Remind with the photo, then remove the usual doubts (shipping, returns, security). Discounts on the first touch train people to abandon.
- **Measure:** Recovered revenue per email
- **Keep:** product photo; reassurances
- **Avoid:** discount in the first email; fake scarcity

**Subject lines to test**

- Still thinking about the {{product_name}}?
- Your cart is saved
- Did something go wrong at checkout?

Slots: `preheader`, `product_image`, `product_name`, `product_detail`, `product_price`, `cta_label`, `cta_url`, `assurance_1`, `assurance_2`, `assurance_3`
