## Google Sans

mailG bundles Google Sans variable Roman and Italic fonts from the official
[Google Sans v14.000 release](https://github.com/googlefonts/googlesans/releases/tag/v14.000).
The original release archive is
[`GoogleSans-v14.000.zip`](https://github.com/googlefonts/googlesans/releases/download/v14.000/GoogleSans-v14.000.zip)
(SHA-256 `d338f0e74f5eabead86a85b87edf4cc50cd6561196dbe173d1db4139e95b3ca7`).
These WOFF2 files were converted without subsetting or outline changes from the
release's `build/GoogleSans/variable/` TTF files. The font supports weight
400–700 and optical size 17–18. Optical size 17 is Google Sans Text; 18 is
Google Sans.

Google Sans is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 without Reserved
Font Names. Its copyright and full license text are in
[`public/fonts/OFL.txt`](public/fonts/OFL.txt), copied from the
[v14.000 source tag](https://github.com/googlefonts/googlesans/blob/v14.000/OFL.txt).

Google and Google Sans are marks of Google LLC. mailG is an independent project
and is not affiliated with Google.
